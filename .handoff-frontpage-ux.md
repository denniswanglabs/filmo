## Key findings (pre-edit)
- The generic-AI "selection ring" Dennis hates = the repeated pattern
  `outline: 2px solid var(--amber); outline-offset: 2px;` applied to EVERY pill/card/option
  on `:focus-visible`, PLUS `.q-opt.is-on` / `.brain-opt.is-on` / `.pace-opt.is-on`

  reads as a browser-default focus ring.

  padding-left:34px. Overlap was marginal; tightened spacing + bumped padding.
