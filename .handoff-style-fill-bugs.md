## What was fixed

### BUG 1 — Truncated headlines (line ~204)

**Before:** `_title_from_text(text, limit=48)` — hard 48-char clip on a word
boundary with no cleanup of trailing commas or dangling function words.
Produced: `"Tripadvisor lets you explore activities, tours,"` and
`"Tripadvisor provides trusted hotel reviews from"` (at limit=48 these were
exactly the cut points).

**After:** `_title_from_text(text, limit=72)` with:
1. Trailing comma stripped (`result.rstrip(",")`)
2. Dangling function word loop — if last word is in `_DANGLING_WORDS`
   (`from`, `to`, `and`, `with`, `at`, `of`, `for`, `the`, `a`, `an`, `in`,
   `on`, `or`, `by`, `as`), it is popped until a content word is the tail.
3. `_DANGLING_WORDS` frozenset added above the function.

Sample output after fix:
- `"Tripadvisor provides trusted hotel reviews from millions of travelers worldwide."` → `"Tripadvisor provides trusted hotel reviews from millions of travelers"`
- `"Tripadvisor lets you explore activities, tours, and experiences at amazing places."` → `"Tripadvisor lets you explore activities, tours, and experiences"`

### BUG 2 — Empty bullets on bot-blocked brands (line ~313)

**Before:** When `brand["features"]` is `[]`, `feats` resolved to `[]` and
`bullets` stayed `[]`, rendering a hollow explainer card with blank lower body.

**After:** A synthesis fallback fires only when `bullets` is still empty after
the feature-lookup block. It:
1. Concatenates `_text + brief + title` as the source.
2. Splits on `[.!?,;—–\n]+` to get raw phrase candidates.
3. Filters out phrases with <3 words or opening with a conjunction/article
   (split artifacts like "and experiences at every destination").
4. Truncates each phrase to 40 chars at a word boundary, drops trailing
   dangling words.
5. Sentence-cases each, dedupes, caps at 3 bullets.

Real features still take priority — the synthesis block only runs when `not bullets`.
