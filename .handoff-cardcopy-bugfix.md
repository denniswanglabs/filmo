## Phase 1 — fixes landed (DONE)

### Bug 1 (HIGH) — apostrophe garble in `_section_label` (~line 411)
Old regex `r"['\"‘’“”]([^...]+)['\"‘’“”]"` matched the possessive apostrophe in
"Figma's" as an opening quote → rendered "S Brand Colors And The Text".
Fix: new extractor that
- only treats straight `'`/`"` as an OPENING quote at a word boundary
  (negative lookbehind `(?<![A-Za-z0-9])`), so possessives are skipped;
- curly quotes match freely (never used for possessives);
- prefers the LAST valid quoted pair (trailing `'Built for teams'` wins);
- requires `[A-Za-z]{2,}` in the capture (rejects a lone "s" remnant);
- robust to MULTIPLE apostrophes before the intended phrase.
Also: explicitly-quoted phrases now PRESERVE author casing (only first letter
upper) instead of `.title()` → renders "Built for teams" (matches the VO), and the
keyword fallback strips a possessive `'s` so no apostrophe-S leaks into a fallback label.

### Bug 2 (MED) — wrong-domain CTA in `_copy_from_brief` (~line 465)
Was hardcoded `"Start building"`. New `_cta_title(brief, name)` derives the CTA
imperative from the CLOSING scene's own brief (in scope — `generate(scene, palette)`
gets the scene; goal is NOT threaded to codegen, so brief is the right source):
strips a "Call to action:/CTA:" scaffold, pulls a leading `<Verb> <obj?>` (verbs:
start/get/try/build/design/create/ship/launch/explore/discover/join/sign/begin/make/plan),
stops the object at the first connector (with/for/at/today/free/your/...), neutral
"Get started" fallback. Figma → "Start designing"; Stripe still → "Start building".

### Bug 3 (LOW) — blank tagline for `_default` palette
Open card subtitle was `palette["tagline"]` which `_default` lacks. New
`_synth_tagline(brief, name)` uses a QUOTED tagline from the open brief if present
(boundary-safe extractor), else neutral "See what you can build". Only quoted phrases
are trusted (no directive-remnant junk). Wired as `tagline or _synth_tagline(...)`,
so kitted brands keep their real palette tagline untouched.
