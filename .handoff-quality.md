## The model (Dennis)
Replace the single `premium_vo` add-on with a STANDARD | PREMIUM quality dimension,
chosen UPFRONT on the composer (it changes what the agent produces):
- STANDARD = Remotion designed motion-graphics + edge-tts VO. NO Higgsfield, NO
  ElevenLabs. Cheapest/deterministic/fast. COGS ~free (Remotion + edge) -> price
  lands at the $5 floor.
- PREMIUM = adds cinematic AI footage via Higgsfield + a natural ElevenLabs VO.
  COGS = Higgsfield clip costs + ElevenLabs VO -> price ~$6-9.

Cost-plus math UNCHANGED: base = max($5 floor, round50(plan_cogs * markup 6)).
The DIFFERENCE is plan COGS: premium's cinematic clips + ElevenLabs land in COGS;
standard zeroes them out.

selection shape: `{ "quality": "standard"|"premium" }`. Back-compat: legacy
`{options:{premium_vo:true}}` -> "premium"; legacy founder_voice booster -> premium.
