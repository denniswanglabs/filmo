## CONTRACT RECONCILIATION (important)
build_timeline.py emits cue at_frame as ABSOLUTE timeline frames (clamped within
[in_frame,out_frame]), NOT scene-relative. A <Sequence from=in_frame> child sees
LOCAL frames, so Timeline.tsx rebases cues to scene-relative at the seam
(rebaseCues). The fixture uses absolute cue frames to match. build_timeline.py
output does NOT carry `theme` or per-scene `data` — those come from the
style-fill engine and are merged into props; the composition reads them straight.
The --props override path was proven (90f dark-theme render overriding the 300f
fixture via calculateMetadata reading total_frames).

## How it consumes timeline.json + theme
- Default = bundled fixture. At render time the orchestrator passes the real
  timeline.json via `remotion render src/index.ts Timeline out.mp4 --props=<file-or-json>`.
  Remotion shallow-merges props over defaultProps; calculateMetadata sets duration/fps.
- theme (palette/fonts/wordmark) is a field INSIDE timeline.json (per brief), read
  straight through to the archetypes.
