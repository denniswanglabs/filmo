## FIX 1 — Numeral mid-clause chop (TripAdvisor D6/D9 residual)

**Root cause:** `_punchy_headline`'s `_BARE_QUANTIFIERS` set only covered plural forms ("millions") and the original guard only fired when the LAST word was a quantifier AND the second-to-last was in `_DANGLING_WORDS`. It missed:
- Singular magnitude words: "million", "billion", "thousand", "hundred"
- Bare numeral tokens: "800", "12,000", "1.3m"
- Quantifier modifiers preceding the number: "over", "about", "approximately"

**What changed:**
- Added singular magnitude words to `_BARE_QUANTIFIERS`: `"million", "billion", "trillion", "thousand", "hundred"`
- Added `_NUMERAL_RE = re.compile(r"^\d[\d,.]*(k|m|b)?$", re.IGNORECASE)` to match bare numeric tokens
- Added `_QUANTIFIER_MODIFIERS` frozenset: `"over", "about", "approximately", "around", "nearly", "roughly", "more", "less", "than", "almost", "exactly", "just", "only"`
- Added `_is_dangling_number_tail(words)` helper that fires when: last word is a bare numeral OR a magnitude word preceded by a function/numeral/quantifier word
- New stripping loop backs up past the ENTIRE dangling number phrase (numeral + magnitude + modifier + function words) to the nearest content word

**Before → After (exact R5 failure):**
- VO beat: `"Tripadvisor gives travelers access to over 800 million trusted reviews"`
- BEFORE: `"Tripadvisor gives travelers access to over 800 million"` (dangling numeral+magnitude)
- AFTER: `"Tripadvisor gives travelers access"` (complete clause ending on content word)

**Preserved (regression guard):**
- `"Stripe processes payments in over 135 currencies"` → stays intact (number has its noun, 48 chars exactly)
- `"Plaid connects apps to over 12,000 banks"` → still distills cleanly (noun "banks" present)
- `"Stripe processes payments in over 135 currencies around the world"` (longer) → "Stripe processes payments" (backs up when noun truncated off)

## FIX 2 — Lowercase/preposition-final subtitle fragment (Shopify D6/D9 residual)

**Root cause:** No validation on subtitles before rendering. Scrape artifacts like "store they line up for" (lowercase-leading, preposition-final) could pass through `_clean_complete_headline` (which only rejects truncated meta-descriptions ≥70 chars or with ellipsis) and render verbatim.

**What changed:**
- Added `_is_fragment_subtitle(text)` predicate (rejecting on: (a) starts lowercase, (b) ends on prep/article/conjunction, (c) single-word)
- Applied guard in `_shape_hero` opening subtitle path → drops fragment, shows empty subtitle
- Applied guard in `_shape_hero` closing subtitle path → drops fragment, falls back to `cta_url`
- Applied guard in `_shape_explainer` subtitle path → drops fragment, shows empty subtitle
- Applied guard in `_brand_fallback_title` → rejects fragment taglines from becoming the display title
- Applied guard in `_distinct_tagline` → rejects fragment taglines before returning them as subtitle candidates

**Before → After (exact R5 failure):**
- Subtitle candidate: `"store they line up for"`
- BEFORE: rendered verbatim as subtitle
- AFTER: `_is_fragment_subtitle("store they line up for")` = True → subtitle dropped (empty)

**Fragment guard is conservative:** Rejects ONLY lowercase-leading, preposition-final, or single-word strings. Valid taglines like "Make Commerce Better For Everyone", "Commerce Made Easy", "For every stage of business." are NOT rejected.
