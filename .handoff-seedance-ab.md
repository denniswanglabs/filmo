## VERDICT — the formula CLEARLY beats the verbatim baseline on real VIDEO. (decisive)

### Frame observations
**BASELINE (verbatim "Clean hero plate of the Stripe Dashboard developer tools"):**
- Renders a flat, literal browser-window screenshot of a Stripe-like dashboard, head-on, filling the frame. Zero cinematography — looks like a screen recording, not a film plate.
- Brand color present (Stripe blue/#635BFF-ish header) but reads as generic SaaS, not art-directed.
- **TEXT GARBLE IS BACK FOR VIDEO.** Headline renders "**Plusiyly be Dos Datas**" (gibberish), stat labels "Successf payments", a giant fake "**SUCCESS**" toast, and a wall of garbled mono-text in the side panel. This is the exact garble the stills review flagged — videotape-alpha's still-text fidelity does NOT carry to Seedance VIDEO. **Confirmed: literal-UI text legibility does NOT hold for video.**
- No negative space, no title room — text would have to overlay directly on busy garbled UI.

**FORMULA (honed cinematic_prompt, establish track):**
- Genuinely cinematic: a floating tablet/glass device rising out of premium dark studio haze, glossy reflection, shallow DoF, brand-accent rim light. Apple-keynote energy. Agency-grade.
- Dashboard UI present but small, drifting in lower third with bokeh — exactly as the prompt steers. Brand-accent #635BFF/#80E9FF picked up in the glowing card chips.
- **Anti-text guard WORKS on video:** no garbled wordmarks/headlines. The UI reads as soft glowing shapes/chips, not legible (broken) text. The garble problem is eliminated by construction.
- Big negative space top + upper area = real title-overlay room. This is a usable brand-film OPENER plate; the baseline is not.
- Motion: slow tasteful drift/parallax of the device + cards, no warping or melt. Holds together for the full 6s.

### Why the formula wins on video specifically
The stills review concluded videotape-alpha renders literal UI text legibly, so the "hero" track skips the anti-text guard. **That conclusion is model-specific and does NOT generalize to Seedance video** — Seedance garbles literal UI text badly under motion. The formula's **anti-text guard + abstract/establishing framing is exactly the right defense for VIDEO**, turning the garble-prone literal-screenshot into a clean, legible-free cinematic plate with title room. The formula's brand-hex injection also keeps Stripe identity without needing (garbled) wordmarks.

## What to improve next (one note)
**Make `detect_kind` route VIDEO models to the `establish` track much more aggressively.** Today `detect_kind` only forces establish for video when hints are mixed/absent; an explicit "hero"/dashboard hint on a *video* scene would pick the hero track (brief + cinematography, NO anti-text) — and this A/B proves that produces garbled literal UI on Seedance. Recommended: for any video model (seedance_2_0 / kling / ultra), the anti-text guard should be ON regardless of hero/establish hints, because literal-text fidelity is a still-only (videotape-alpha) property. Simplest patch: in `detect_kind`, when the model is a video model, never return "hero" — always "establish" (or add a third "video-hero" track that keeps cinematography + brand-hex but force-includes the anti-text clause + title room). This is the single highest-leverage change for real video quality. (Read-only this iteration; did not edit.)
