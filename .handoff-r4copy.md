     ideas) — left as-is.
2. **Display headlines — `style_fill` (load-bearing, fully deterministic).**
   - New `_is_weak_headline()` + `_BARE_NOUN_HEADLINES` set: rejects bare category
     nouns ("Products", "Pricing", ...) and 1-word fragments as on-card titles.
   - New `_punchy_headline()`: distills a punchy phrase from a long spoken line —
     strips a trailing CTA tail that points at a DOMAIN ("at stripe.com",
     "with Plaid at plaid.com") + a temporal marker ("today"/"now"), then clamps to a
     word boundary. CONSERVATIVE: a descriptive `with`-clause with no domain
     ("with over 800 million reviews") is preserved, so opening lines stay complete.
   - `_distinct_tagline()`: now rejects weak bare-noun feature labels (so Plaid's
     "Products" can't win); falls to the next real feature phrase.
   - `_shape_hero()`: closing CTA title now uses `_punchy_headline(threaded VO)`
     instead of the mid-URL truncation; opening title gets a FINAL weak-headline
     guard that distills a real phrase from the opening VO / tagline.
