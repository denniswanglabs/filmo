### Fix 2 — No mid-clause chop on headlines

In `_punchy_headline`, extended the guard with `_BARE_QUANTIFIERS` set. After both
the regular truncation pass and the dangling-word strip, checks if the second-to-last
word is in `_DANGLING_WORDS` AND the last word is in `_BARE_QUANTIFIERS` (words like
"millions", "thousands", "many", "some" that form incomplete tails). If so, backs up
past both to the prior content word.

This fires on both:
- Sub-limit text (VO beat literally ends "...access to millions")
- Post-truncation text where the limit clamp exposed the quantifier
