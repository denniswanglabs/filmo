## Cross-brand pattern (now 9 brands, 4 measured for hue accuracy)
The v3/v4 data shows a consistent rule: **color-DOMINANCE reliably unifies the whole frame to one color
(always beats the old chaos), and hue ACCURACY tracks how close the brand hex sits to the model's
prototype for the chosen NAME.**

  Shopify olive→emerald (~35°), Twitch indigo→blue (~39°). v4 cut Shopify's miss in half (74°→35°) by
  using a finer name + paired hex, proving the lever direction is right; the residual is the model's
  prototype skew for the words themselves.
- **Next lever for exactness (pure prompt, future PAID iter):** hue-fence phrasing ("a warm yellow-green,
  NOT a bright emerald" / "a violet-leaning purple, NOT a blue") or a name nudged toward the brand's
  off-axis lean. Validate each with ONE re-roll. Not done here (read-only + budget).
